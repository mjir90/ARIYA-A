# Preliminary imagery review deliverables

These files are a preliminary technical review of two KML polygons and 14 Google Earth screen captures supplied for the Mahan area.

- The PDFs are **not a final signed expert opinion**. Image screenshots are not raw georeferenced rasters; no cadastral evidence, ground control, or field survey was supplied.
- Dates in the inventory are the historical-view labels displayed in Google Earth. They were not independently verified as exact sensor acquisition dates.
- KML polygon areas calculated geodesically on WGS84: `m1` 7,574.3 m²; `m2` 3,908.1 m²; combined 11,482.3 m². These describe the supplied KML geometries, not verified cadastral areas or legal ownership.
- Screenshot-to-screenshot feature registration is relative only and does not establish absolute positional accuracy. No parcel-level occupation or land-use change has been asserted.
- The supplied set spans displayed dates 2003-01-13 through 2024-07-21. No verified imagery was added for 1990–2002 or after 2024.
- `فهرست_تصاویر_مرتب.csv` lists the source captures and date-based names. `registration_QA.json` records the relative alignment QA.
- `تصاویر_مرتب_تاریخی_و_KML.zip` contains the date-renamed copies and original KMLs in the workspace deliverables, but is intentionally excluded from Git because it contains the approximately 37.5 MB image dataset.
